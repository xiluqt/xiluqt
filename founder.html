The company

Not another dashboard.

Xiluqt is being developed as an AI infrastructure and movement-intelligence company. The prototype combines a World Graph, predictive risk, digital twins, control-tower concepts, document intelligence, freight and inventory intelligence, an AI analyst, trust controls and an API-first architecture.

The unusual part

Starting before everything is perfect.

At 19, the founder is not starting with a giant campus, a huge engineering department or a proprietary global data network. He is starting with a working prototype, a public demonstration and a thesis: real-world intelligence should become an infrastructure layer, not another isolated dashboard.

And the prototype is honest about its boundaries. The compact local model uses synthetic development scenarios; it is not presented as production-grade performance. The API is marked BETA PROTOTYPE. The next leap requires funding, authorized customer data, licensed external data and production infrastructure.
