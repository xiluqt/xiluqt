Why customers can benefit

The economic thesis is simple: earlier detection of delay, disruption or operational friction can create opportunities to reroute, rebalance inventory, intervene with carriers, or protect service levels.

  • Earlier risk visibility
  • Prioritized interventions
  • Continuous outcome measurement
  • Customer-specific model improvement
  • API access for operational systems

What we will measure

Xiluqt should earn trust through evidence, not marketing claims.

  • Prediction calibration and accuracy
  • False-positive / false-negative rates
  • Lead time before operational events
  • Intervention success rate
  • Documented customer ROI

Important commercial principle

Xiluqt should never guarantee a particular financial outcome or model accuracy without validated evidence. Enterprise contracts should define the data supplied, measurement methodology, acceptance thresholds, security obligations, service levels, and exactly which milestones trigger any deferred payment.
