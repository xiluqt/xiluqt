Xiluqt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

What Xiluqt processes

Xiluqt may process logistics prediction inputs, operational signals, and user-provided prompts when those features are used. The browser prototype may store learning and outcome records locally on the device.

Local-first processing

Where Xiluqt uses a local model such as Ollama, prompts can remain on the local machine. Local storage is controlled by the device and browser.

Hosted MCP

If you connect to a hosted Xiluqt MCP endpoint, requests are transmitted to that server so it can execute the requested read-only intelligence tools. Deployment operators may process request metadata such as IP address, timestamps, and request identifiers for security and reliability.

No autonomous consequential actions

The published MCP toolset is designed for read-only prediction, explanation, status, and optional reasoning. It does not authorize payments, shipment mutations, credential operations, or other consequential external writes.

Data retention

Local browser records remain under the user's device storage controls. Hosted retention depends on the deployment operator and configuration. Do not send secrets or sensitive personal information to a public endpoint.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding a particular Xiluqt deployment, contact the operator of that deployment.
